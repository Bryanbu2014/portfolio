Paper Trading Lab

A simulated market environment for backtesting and evaluating trading strategies.

View Source

Introduction

This is a clean, fast virtual stock trading terminal built with Python and Streamlit.

Paper Trading Lab allows users to practice stock trading using live market prices without risking real money. Users get a virtual $100,000 starting capital, and all their trades, portfolios, and watchlists are saved to a cloud database.

Motivation

The idea for this Paper Trading Lab stemmed from a desire to learn and understand market dynamics and to test swing trading strategies without losing real money on the stock market. I wanted to build a realistic, data-driven sandbox environment where I could apply analytical thinking to real-time scenarios. Beyond just practicing trading, this project was built to serve as a foundation platform to explore how data analysis and AI can be effectively used to evaluate, refine, and support decision making.

Features

  • Secure Login: Private accounts so only you can access your portfolio.
  • Cloud Saves: Your balance, trades, and watchlists are backed up to a Supabase database. You can close the app and pick up right where you left off.
  • Live Data: Real-time stock prices fetched using Yahoo Finance (yfinance).
  • Portfolio Tracking: Automatically calculates your total shares and average buy prices.
  • Watchlist: Add and track live prices for your favorite stocks.
  • Trade Journal: Keeps a full history of every buy and sell order.

Tech Stack

  • UI & Backend: Streamlit
  • Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
  • Market Data: yfinance
  • Data Handling: pandas

Status: On Hold, Rethinking the Approach

The original plan was an AI layer on top of the paper trading terminal: portfolio analysis and buy/sell suggestions for my own day trading. I stopped before building it: short-term price movements are too unpredictable for reliable signals, and I didn't want a tool whose core feature I couldn't trust. I'm now exploring how AI can support trading decisions without depending on price prediction.
