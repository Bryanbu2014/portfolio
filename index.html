About Me

I'm a Malaysian graduate of the Deggendorf Institute of Technology, where I completed my Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence in 2026. I now work as an AI Engineer at Toolbox Cloud Solutions. My passion lies in the field of Natural Language Processing, inspired by the rise of large language model (LLM). Throughout my academic journey, I find myself fascinated by the intricate ways in which LLMs can interpret, generate, and transform human language. Amazing, isn't it? This fascination has led me to seek out various projects related to the field.

Through my academic path and internship role at Bosch, I have built a robust toolkit in Python, Computer Vision, and Data Analysis with cloud-based solutions. During my internship, I specialized in cloud-based data pipelines to process large and real-time datasets. My work focused on generating insights for defect detection and designing interactive dashboards to visualize these results for industrial decision-making.

Building on top of this foundation, my Bachelor's thesis at Bosch applied Computer Vision (YOLO11) to defect detection on electric motors, with an edge setup on a Raspberry Pi 5, evaluated against production-line requirements. The thesis aimed to bridge the gap between research and production-ready industrial applications.

Just a brief section about my hobby to get to know more about me. Besides studies, I really enjoy music, especially playing the piano. For me, it's a fun way to relax and it also helps me to be more creative and focused, which I believe is beneficial for everything I do, including my studies and projects.
